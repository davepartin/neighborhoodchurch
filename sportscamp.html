What Is GO Sports Camp?

GO Sports Camp is our FREE three-day neighborhood camp for kids entering K–7th grade. Every morning is packed with soccer, basketball, and cheer, plus games, snacks, and stories about the love of Jesus. It's our favorite week of the summer — here's a quick taste of the energy and heart behind it all.
